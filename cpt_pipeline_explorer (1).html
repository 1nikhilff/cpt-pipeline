Positive Anchors
Rare semantic reinforcement
▶

Negative Anchors
Wrong medical territory detection
▶
Negative anchors are distractor words. These are words appearing frequently inside candidate CPT codes but absent from the device description. They represent the wrong medical territory. Evaluation Process: • System examines all semantic search candidates. • Finds words appearing in ≥45% of returned CPT candidates. • Checks whether those words are absent from device description. • If absent: that word becomes a Negative Anchor. Example: Searching for "needle" may retrieve many biopsy codes. If device is a "suture needle" and never mentions biopsy: "biopsy" becomes a negative anchor. Those CPT codes are penalized.
Negative Anchor Rule:
